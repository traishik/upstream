## Why I built this

*[Your story goes here. A starting draft:]*

I've been on both sides of the hiring table. I've written job descriptions, sat in debriefs, and also refreshed my inbox waiting for replies that never came. The part that wore me down wasn't rejection. It was not knowing whether the role I'd spent an evening on was even real.

So I built the list I wished I had: every role straight from the company's own careers page, checked to be live, with one button that takes you straight to the source. No reposts, no ghosts, no middlemen. Just a calmer way to look for your next step.

If it helps you, tell me. If something's missing, tell me that too. This is built for the community, and it gets better because of you.
